## Summary

A central hub for various loss functions used in Deep Learning, ranging from standard classification losses to specialized regularization techniques.
## Classification
- [Binary Cross-Entropy Loss](/atlas/ai/deep-learning/loss-functions/binary-cross-entropy-loss) - Standard for binary classification.
- [Cross-Entropy Loss](/atlas/ai/deep-learning/loss-functions/cross-entropy-loss) - Standard for multi-class classification, usually paired with Softmax.

## Regression & Distance
- [L2 Norm and Loss](/atlas/ai/deep-learning/loss-functions/l2-norm-and-loss) - Mean Squared Error and Euclidean distance.

## Policy & Reinforcement Learning
- [Group Relative Policy Optimization](/atlas/ai/deep-learning/loss-functions/group-relative-policy-optimization) (GRPO) - Used in modern RLHF/Reasoning models (e.g., DeepSeek-R1).

## Representation Learning & Regularization
- [LeJEPA](/atlas/ai/deep-learning/loss-functions/lejepa) - Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture loss.
- [Sketched Isotropic Gaussian Regularization](/atlas/ai/deep-learning/loss-functions/sketched-isotropic-gaussian-regularization) (SIGReg) - Anti-collapse regularization for JEPAs.
