# Atlas

This is my structured notebook for concepts I want to keep close: artificial intelligence, mathematics, computer systems, algorithms, security, and tooling.

The goal is not to mirror papers or documentation. The goal is to turn useful ideas into notes that are compact enough to revisit and precise enough to build from.

## Main Areas

- [AI](/atlas/ai/foundations/attention-mechanism): architectures, training, inference, optimization, model reports, and foundations.
- [Mathematics](/atlas/math/probability/entropy): probability, statistics, calculus, and linear algebra notes used by the AI material.
- [Systems](/atlas/systems/performance/roofline-model): infrastructure, distributed systems, performance, networking, and parallel computing.
- [Algorithms](/atlas/algorithms/patterns/top-k-pattern): data structures, interview patterns, and algorithmic primitives.
- [Tooling](/atlas/tooling/profiling/nsys-profiler): debugging, profiling, and network analysis.

## Reading Style

Most notes are written as working memory:

- definitions first,
- formulas when they clarify,
- practical consequences after the theory,
- links to adjacent notes when the concept depends on another one.

Start anywhere. The Atlas is meant to be navigated by curiosity, not read in order.
