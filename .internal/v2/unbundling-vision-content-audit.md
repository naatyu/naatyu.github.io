## Teaching spine and scientific boundaries

1. Separate visual prior, dedicated vision capacity and location relative to fusion. Encoder-free still has a small patch embedding; “no dedicated capacity” means no substantial dedicated module of the tested size.
2. Retain the matched-data/compute scope: common sources, not identical token exposure. Main 1.7B/4B comparisons fix 0.4B visual capacity; the separate 0.6B–6.4B ladder grows it with the backbone. Encoder objective comparisons and free-pretraining comparisons have different compute denominators.
3. Explain additive Q/K/V/O and FFN branches: every token keeps the shared path, image tokens add modality-specific contributions, attention runs once, masks retain image/document/causal boundaries, FFNs read the same normalized post-attention input. The modality indicator is not learned expert selection. Zero initialization applies to additional attention matrices and the FFN output projection, not every FFN matrix. Shared weights can still change during training on multimodal data.
4. Keep discrete placement evidence, including Avg. VQA/OCR/Text and encoder/encoder-free references. Equal parameters and compute do not guarantee equal token exposure. “Within one point” is a table-highlighting convention, not statistical equivalence; causal explanations of early specialization or modality competition remain hypotheses.
5. Preserve the compute definition: algorithmic training FLOPs, excluding common instruction-tuning evaluation, recomputation, optimizer/communication/input/elementwise work. Token-equivalents and non-embedding TPP are not literal processed-token counts. Shared Qwen2.5-VL recaptions remain an external teacher contribution to the common data pipeline.
6. Preserve evaluation differences: 224px/256 patches in multimodal pretraining, 448px/1,024 patches plus Cambrian-7M ×3 before VQA, text evaluation before that tuning, ImageNet probes of an upstream representation. Additional images in released encoders are not controlled by this study.
7. Separate caption-loss signal from averaged validation loss, LR ablations from architectural evidence, and serving medians/ranges from production claims. TTFT uses paper Table 36 (1.7B/4B, H200, sixteen concurrent one-image requests); four timing runs are not multiple training seeds. Eager implementation and unoptimized encoder affect interpretation. Dense scale, resolution, interleaving, video and MoE limitations remain explicit.
