## Corrections / qualifications

- Llama b=500,000 supports phase choices; 128k also requires staged training. Larger base is not a context guarantee and leaves pair zero unchanged.
- Existing piecewise ReRoPE expression is a local/distant illustrative warp, not an exact method/kernel implementation. Preserve signed displacement and explain non-additive composition and cache incompatibility.
- A nonsingular same-time map is required before normalizing to identity through invertible basis transformations. Singular maps cannot be made invertible by a basis change.
- Decay uses nonnegative key age; key-minus-query changes sign. General groups use distinct inverse query/key maps. Orthogonal rotations permit the shared transform.
- Imaginary diagonalizable spectra give bounded motion in a fixed basis, not necessarily norm preservation. Skew symmetry gives orthogonality. Non-normal/ill-conditioned systems require additional numerical care.
- NoPE does not erase masks/order or shrink full attention/KV. Partial RoPE and MLA's unrotated subspace are not pure NoPE.
