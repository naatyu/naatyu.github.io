## Corrections and conditions

- CDF right-continuity and atoms' left limits added. Absolute continuity—not mere continuity—is required for a density that reconstructs the CDF by integration.
- Discrete quantiles use a generalized inverse. They do not need exact equality F(x)=u. Library interpolation/boundary conventions matter.
- Finite absolute moments justify the displayed derivative exchange; characteristic functions always existing does not give finite moments.
- The full characteristic function determines a distribution; selected frequencies do not. “Small/global, large/local” remains an intuition with limits.
- Zero covariance is not independence; an X² counterexample is worked. Identity covariance alone is not Gaussianity or a complete account of SIGReg.
- Sensitivity is not posterior probability. Diagnostic example explicitly hypothetical, not health guidance.
- Test statistic versus p-value and optimization regularizer versus calibrated test are separate. No new benchmark or p-value claimed.
