## Corrections and evidence

Fixed-window state has at most k items during startup, exactly k afterward. Negative values do not break fixed additive updates, but can break usual variable sum validity. Exactly(K) subtraction applies to counts, not optimum lengths. Sum-bound sorted pairs require a sorted input, distinct indices and retained original-index policy when sorting. Each pointer's monotone movement does not make all center/outer loops linear. Map size represents distinct keys only after zero counts are removed. A deque, not merely a stack, supports expired sliding-maximum candidates.

These are derivations from the user-owned patterns and exact new examples, not a claim of a fresh complete algorithm-textbook review.
