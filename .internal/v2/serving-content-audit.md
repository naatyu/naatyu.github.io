## Corrections and verification limits

1. First output can be sampled from final-prefill logits. The inherited extra-first-decode term depends on engine accounting; do not require or double-count it.
2. Derive Bcrit=Mw*C/(2P*W), then(qw/2)*C/W with bytes/weight and conventional FLOPs/s. The inherited width-ratio shorthand hides conventions; activation bytes do not independently enter that equality. Neither expression is an empirical optimum.
3. Additive KV-plus-linear accounting omits attentioncompute/synchronization; not a universal rigorous sum bound.
4. Storage sharing is not necessarily read-traffic sharing. Payload size is not physical paging overhead.
5. Prefix identity includes entire earlier history/model/cache contract, not isolated equal blocks.
6. Infrastructure orchestration does not automatically infer semantic task difficulty or prove scale.
7. TTS N/RTF is ideal aggregate capacity, not observed concurrent deadline performance; nominal VRAM is not free stream state.
8. Component FLOPs, cache reductions and acceptance lengths are not end-to-end speedups. Baseline/hardware caveats stay attached.
