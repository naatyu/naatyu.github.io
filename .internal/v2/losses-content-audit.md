## Corrected source statements

- BCE diverges logarithmically in the inverse correct-class probability, not exponentially.
- Natural-log loss uses nats. Zero hard-label loss is a limiting value for finite logits; soft labels retain entropy.
- L2 norm is not globally strictly convex as a function: it is linear along positive rays. Squared residual convexity does not imply neural-parameter convexity.
- Sum squared residuals is not MSE without a denominator.
- Fixed-variance Gaussian NLL gives squared error; learned variance also requires log σ.
- L2 normalization does not prevent collapse: identical unit vectors are collapsed.
- “Inverse L2 loss” is not a standard inverse operation. Negative log-likelihood is not reciprocal likelihood, and the ridge solve is a separate linear-algebra result.
- Local log-score uniqueness needs alphabet-size/regularity conditions; binary exceptions exist.
- Soft-margin support vectors may lie inside the margin or on the wrong side, not only on its boundary.
- FY includes −H(p) for a soft target. A matched FY loss is not Brier arbitrarily composed with softmax.
- Separate Bernoulli losses estimate marginal labels; they do not prove data-label independence. Positive-class weighting affects calibration.
- Distributed rank-mean averaging and global token-mean averaging differ when valid-token counts differ.
