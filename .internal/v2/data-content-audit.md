## Corrections and fresh evidence

- Deduplication removes redundant corpus exposure; it does not create novelty or inherently widen raw-minus-unique counts. GLM's reported +28% requires its original denominator/replacement scope.
- Low NLL is not by itself proof of memorization. Similarity and teacher agreement are selection signals, not truth or absence of contamination.
- Token sets/shingles and semantic neighbors can join contradictions. Candidate/review distinction and transitive-cluster risk are explicit.
- Sampling weights, input tokens, supervised targets, loss reductions and gradient contributions differ. A masked turn remains context; target masking does not necessarily detach contextual gradients.
- Mixture search need not always scalarize; weighted sums can miss nonconvex Pareto regions. Priors/caps/surrogates encode assumptions rather than validate performance.
- MAI's rounded percentages sum to 100.1%; no implicit exact renormalization is presented as the published recipe.
- Llama 3.1 report final mix is approximately 50/25/17/8, not the original 95/5 language summary. The report's 40B annealing data experiment and 40M final-run wording are different procedures.
- Qwen2.5 40/60 current-max/short sequence mix belongs to Turbo's progressive stages. Do not apply it indiscriminately to the full family.
- Equal task/harness completion targets do not imply equal tokens, compute, advantages or update contributions. Proposed harness-family weighting is not a reported finding.
