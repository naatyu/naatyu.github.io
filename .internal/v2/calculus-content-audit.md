## Corrections

Steepest gradient direction is specified under a Euclidean norm constraint. Zero gradient has no unit direction and does not imply every optimizer stops. Positive Hessian classifies a stationary point, not any point; semidefinite curvature is not sufficient. A negative direction rules out a minimum but a nondegenerate saddle needs both signs. Minibatch gradients need not vanish at a population optimum. Clipping zero handling and actual update versus gradient are separate. Conditional Hessian statistics include determinant weighting; unconditional GOE draws are not equivalent to a uniformly counted critical point.
