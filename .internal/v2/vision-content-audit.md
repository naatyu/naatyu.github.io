### Mathematical correction found in LeJEPA v3

The paper'sEq6→7 andAppendixB.6Eq23–26 identify mean squared global norm with squared global mean norm. That equality omits global-view variance. For global embeddings−1,+1 andallviews−1,+1,centeredMSE=1whilepairwiseglobal-to-allMSE=2. The correct identity is:

`mean(g,a)||g−a||² = mean(a)||mean(g)−a||² + mean(g)||g−mean(g)||²`.

Use the implemented centeredloss as its owndeclaredobjective, not an exact equivalent ofthepairwiseloss. The omittedterm is not generallyconstant withrespect tosharedencoderparameters. Newmodelchecks fullcentergradientbyfinite differences andthe exactdecomposition. This is a direct algebraiccheck; not a claimthatallLeJEPAresults are invalid. Futurepublicreaderneeds a shortprecisionnote,not a derailment ofthemainmechanism.
