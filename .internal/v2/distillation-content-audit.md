## Corrections and exact contracts

These are mathematical/editorial corrections, not claims that a new full primary-paper audit is complete.

- A conditional reverse KL value is E[a~p](log p−log q). With the sampled token held fixed, directly differentiating log p−log q yields an expected score gradient of zero. The correct fixed-prefix gradient is E[(log p−log q)∇log p]; the omitted +1 baseline has zero expectation.
- A detached advantage log q−log p_behavior inside a policy-gradient surrogate gives that conditional gradient at the matching behavior/current policy. Off-policy collection, importance weighting and clipping change the estimator; do not promise exact equality under arbitrary replay.
- A full sequence reverse KL differentiates the distribution of prefixes too. Immediate-token feedback or stopped-prefix GKD is a deliberate local update, not generally the full sequence gradient. A two-token enumeration demonstrates the missing visitation term exactly.
- GKD can use forward KL on student-generated prefixes. “On-policy” specifies the collected histories, not the divergence direction.
- Forward KL versus reverse KL changes the weighting of teacher/student mass. Mode-seeking shorthand is about approximation under constraints, not an inevitable collapse theorem.
- Temperature-squared weighting gives derivative τ(p−q) with respect to unscaled student logits. It is approximate gradient-scale compensation at high temperature, not exact temperature-independent training.
- Top-k renormalization discards omitted probability mass and produces a conditional distribution. A remainder bucket retains that mass but not its detailed token allocation.
- Teacher agreement is not task correctness. Wrong-prefix continuations, teacher bias, finite coverage and normalization conventions remain important.
- Self-sampled finite-batch SFT has zero initial gradient in expectation, but a realized batch can update the model. Repeated fitting becomes off-policy. This explains a possible drift mechanism; it does not prove universal task degradation.
- Full-vocabulary teacher probabilities and sampled-token log probabilities are distinct feedback contracts. Do not claim the richer target necessarily wins every task.
- Self-distillation can reset weights/optimizer from another checkpoint while extracting behavior. It does not fix the cause of a failed run automatically.
