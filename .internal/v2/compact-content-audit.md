## Corrections

- Embeddings are not useless for reasoning. V×d storage and an untied output matrix consume budget while supplying input/output representations.
- The 28T Liquid figure belongs to LFM2.5-350M, not every LFM2 release. Unsourced 63%/90% interview ratios are not generalized.
- Nanbeige's named 3B denominator differs from rounded 4B stored inventory. Keep both conventions; TPP is not FLOPs.
- Offline answer refinement is not architectural recurrence. Two shared-weight passes still require the released two sets of pass-indexed cache state.
- Masked imitation loss does not imply no indirect gradient through the context.
- Strict binary pass@16 fraction 10–90% retains 2–14 successes. pass@8 is a different statistic.
- Length control requires a defined correct-history fallback and maximum above the median. Clipping the penalty does not itself stop generation.
- Gemma's published “Gb” caption is flagged rather than silently relabeled; effective/active footprints are not proof of total resident storage. Hardware/model/encoder changes confound quantization-only attribution.
- Apple's unconditional historical private-interaction claim is no longer current: the overview has an opt-in exception.
