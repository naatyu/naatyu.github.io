## Mathematical corrections and tested foundations

agents-model.js is an exact constructed teaching model, not a reproduction of the reports' full systems.

- executableTask checks all four target/regression conditions.
- rubricRewards applies binary correctness×solution×behavior and centers each group.
- redistributeGroup corrects confirmed hacks before computing centered binary advantages. It computes uncapped λ, applied cap, pre-center mass, mean shift and final advantages separately.
- **GAR conservation correction:** uncapped λ preserves original positive advantage mass. An active cap generally loses that guarantee. Subsequent mean subtraction restores zero sum, not necessarily the original positive mass. All-pass/all-fail groups have zero binary advantage; GAR cannot invent a ranking signal in those groups.
- localCredit normalizes token weights to a declared per-response advantage mass. Positive all-flagged tokens have no clean destination: the function reports unallocated mass rather than pretending conservation. For negative all-flagged tokens, uniform amplification cancels after normalization; there is no relative reallocation.
- harnessMass exposes response- versus token-weighted objective shares. These are scalar coefficient masses, not actual vector gradient norms or learning influence.
- completionMixture counts exact deterministic worker completions by a selected horizon and computes long-run rates concurrency/duration. It demonstrates completion-time bias without inventing measured latency/throughput.
- familyMixture multiplies family and conditional harness probabilities. This is the user's coherent follow-up idea, not verified FineEnvs implementation.
- correctnessEfficiency gates a conceptual bounded bonus by success.
- prefixCapture links exact prior full-token prefixes under a stated longest-match teaching tie rule. Retained branches alone contribute declared completion targets. This is not claimed to replicate the vendor's graph algorithm or reward propagation.
- recoveryState retains an invalid assistant action and its tool observation as context while later correct assistant actions supply targets. Prompt/tool tokens never become policy targets.
