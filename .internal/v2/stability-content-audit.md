## Corrections and primary-read scope

- Gradient norm is not general model quality, actual adaptive update size or a monotone convergence certificate. Loss is not a direction.
- Global clipping preserves a nonzero gradient direction, not necessarily the optimizer step. At a zero threshold, direction is undefined. Value/per-layer clipping have different constraints.
- Frequent clipping is not automatically a wrong threshold. Initial 1.0/0.5 suggestions are recipe priors, not universal safe choices.
- Clip after accumulated, synchronized, unscaled gradients. Unequal microbatch target counts need the correct reduction. Nonfinite values need explicit fail/skip policy; clipping is not a repair operation.
- A curve's appearance does not identify the triggering batch or establish irreversible parameter damage. Compare branches and restored data lineage.
- Mean loss can move solely through mixture shares. Different domain losses do not alone rank data quality.
- Mode-hopping is behavioral; no causal circuit destruction established. Single-step and five-checkpoint results have tested-recipe scope. Figure 11 is averaging; adjacent source prose links Figure 12 inconsistently.
- Earlier bases are not universally better. Pairwise answer preference is not unrestricted generation accuracy; sampling repetitions do not expand the five unique persona questions.
